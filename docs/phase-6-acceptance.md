## Limits that remain

Twin copies a workspace; it does not sandbox commands or roll back outside-project writes. Watched paths and an explicitly configured npm prefix have bounded observation, and escaped process descendants are outside confirmed group settlement. Endpoint equality does not prove uninterrupted preservation. The five score fields remain unknown where evidence is insufficient, including documentation accuracy without a version-matched claim. Execution digests support reproduction, not authenticated provenance. The hosted workflow retains uploaded artifacts for 14 days; the committed result remains available separately. Incomplete publication can still fail to produce an artifact, and hosted success at these commits does not guarantee future runs.

The [Codex/Claude matrix](compatibility-matrix.md) retains Claude's model-backed edit, apply-after-edit, and interruption cells as unverified under the optional indefinite budget hold. No paid agent call was part of Phase 6 acceptance.
